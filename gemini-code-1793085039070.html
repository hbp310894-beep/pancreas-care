Comprehensive Clinical Scope

Pancreatic Conditions Evaluated & Managed

Precision diagnostics and individualized treatment pathways tailored to acute pathophysiology, structural ductal changes, and malignant risk factors.

◉

Acute Pancreatitis

Sudden parenchymal autodigestion ranging from mild interstitial edematous inflammation to severe necrotizing pancreatitis requiring multi-system ICU stabilization.

  • Etiology Investigation: Choledocholithiasis, hypertriglyceridemia, alcohol, hypercalcemia, and drug-induced etiologies.
  • Organ Rescue Protocols: Early aggressive goal-directed crystalloid hydration and enteral micro-feeding.
  • Biliary Intervention: Emergency ERCP sphincterotomy within 24–48 hours for concomitant acute cholangitis.
↻

Chronic Pancreatitis

Progressive fibro-inflammatory destruction leading to permanent parenchymal loss, ductal calcifications, disabling neural pain, and metabolic insufficiency.

  • Ductal Obstruction: Extracorporeal shock wave lithotripsy (ESWL) followed by ERCP stone retrieval.
  • Stricture Dilatation: Serial high-caliber plastic or self-expanding covered metal stenting across strictures.
  • Exocrine & Endocrine Care: Weight-adjusted Pancreatic Enzyme Replacement Therapy (PERT) and brittle diabetes control.
◇

Pancreatic Cysts & IPMNs

High-resolution differentiation between benign serous cystadenomas and premalignant mucinous lesions (IPMN, Mucinous Cystic Neoplasm).

  • High-Resolution EUS: Micro-cystic vs. macro-cystic delineation, mural nodule sizing, and septal evaluation.
  • Cyst Fluid Analysis: CEA, amylase, glucose, and cytology to classify mucinous potential.
  • Fukuoka/AGA Guidelines: Structured surveillance intervals to prevent unnecessary resections.
⇄

Walled-Off Necrosis (WON)

Encapsulated collections of necrotic pancreatic parenchymal and peripancreatic tissue developing 4 or more weeks after necrotizing pancreatitis.

  • Step-Up Approach: Conservative stabilization followed by minimally invasive transluminal drainage.
  • LAMS Stenting: Large-diameter lumen-apposing metal stents (Hot-Axios) placed under direct EUS visualization.
  • Direct Necrosectomy (DEN): Serial endoscopic debridement of infected solid non-vascular debris.
◎

Pancreatic Tumors & NETs

Rapid diagnostic work-up and tissue acquisition for ductal adenocarcinoma, solid pseudopapillary neoplasms, and functional/non-functional neuroendocrine tumors.

  • Core Biopsy (FNB): 22G/25G Franseen or fork-tip needle cores for Ki-67 proliferation and immunohistochemistry.
  • Vascular Staging: Multi-detector CT/MRI and EUS assessment of celiac axis and mesenteric vessels.
  • Tumor Board Review: Coordinated with HPB surgical oncology and systemic chemotherapy teams.
⌁

Disconnected Duct Syndrome

Full-thickness transection of the main pancreatic duct caused by central necrosis, resulting in viable isolated upstream tissue that continues secreting.

  • Pathology Recognition: Persistent non-healing fluid collections or pancreaticocutaneous fistulae.
  • Internal Bridging: Long-term indwelling double-pigtail plastic stents placed through the stomach wall.
  • Surgical Sparing: Long-term internal transluminal drainage avoiding distal pancreatectomy.
Interventional Suite Capabilities

Advanced Endoscopic Procedures

State-of-the-art procedures performed in specialized digital fluoroscopy and therapeutic echoendoscopy theaters at Medanta Gurugram.

EUS Cystogastrostomy & Pseudocyst Drainage Therapeutic EUS

Using real-time curvilinear echoendoscopic imaging with color Doppler flow mapping, an unobstructed needle trajectory from the stomach or duodenal bulb into the fluid collection is established under continuous visualization, followed by cautery-enhanced stenting.

Clinical Value: Eliminates external surgical drains, dramatically reduces secondary infection rates, and provides complete internal resolution with minimal hospital stay.

Direct Endoscopic Necrosectomy (DEN) Advanced Transluminal

For patients with infected pancreatic necrosis, a high-caliber lumen-apposing stent provides a conduit through which a gastroscope enters directly into the retroperitoneal necrotic cavity. Debridement is executed using snares, Roth nets, and water-jet irrigation.

Clinical Value: Replaces traumatic open surgical necrosectomy, reduces organ failure exacerbation, and accelerates recovery in critically ill patients.

Therapeutic Pancreatic ERCP Fluoroscopic Access

Cannulation of the minor and major duodenal papillae with selective guidewire cannulation of the main pancreatic duct (Wirsung). Includes high-pressure balloon dilatation of tight strictures, pancreatic sphincterotomy, and clearance of intraductal calculi.

Clinical Value: Re-establishes physiological pancreatic drainage, relieves severe chronic intraductal hypertension, and prevents recurrent acute flare-ups.

EUS-Guided Fine Needle Biopsy (FNB) Histopathology

Using next-generation geometry needles (Franseen and fork-tip core needles), architectural tissue cores are obtained from solid pancreatic head/body/tail lesions without breaching abdominal skin, offering immediate Rapid On-Site Evaluation (ROSE).

Clinical Value: Achieves diagnostic sensitivity exceeding 95% for malignancy, allowing immediate immunohistochemical profiling and targeted oncological therapy.

EUS-Guided Radiofrequency Ablation (RFA) Ablative Therapy

A specialized 19G needle electrode delivers targeted high-frequency thermal energy directly into neuroendocrine tumors (insulinomas, non-functioning NETs) or selected locally advanced cystic lesions under direct sonographic control with internal cooling.

Clinical Value: Offers a parenchymal-sparing, non-surgical alternative for patients with small functional NETs or poor candidates for major pancreatic resection.

Celiac Plexus Neurolysis & Block (CPN/CPB) Targeted Pain Care

Sonographically guided injection of bupivacaine and absolute ethanol directly into the pre-aortic celiac ganglia at the takeoff of the celiac artery to chemically ablate autonomic nociceptive transmission from the pancreas.

Clinical Value: Dramatically reduces narcotic dependency, treats intractable malignant and chronic pain, and mitigates debilitating opioid-induced constipation.

EUS-Guided Coil & Cyanoacrylate Glue Injection Vascular Intervention

Sinistral or left-sided portal hypertension caused by splenic vein thrombosis in pancreatitis can trigger life-threatening fundal gastric varices. Synthetic micro-coils followed by N-butyl-2-cyanoacrylate glue are deployed under endosonographic control.

Clinical Value: Significantly reduces distant pulmonary glue embolization compared to blind endoscopic injection while securing rapid hemostatic variceal obliteration.

Pancreatic Exocrine & Metabolic Optimization Longitudinal Care

Systematic screening for steatorrhea, fat-soluble vitamin malabsorption (A, D, E, K), and early endocrine failure. Protocols include enteric-coated enzyme dosing timed with meal composition and structured management of pancreatogenic (Type 3c) diabetes.

Clinical Value: Prevents progressive osteopenia, reverses severe involuntary weight loss, and stabilizes brittle postprandial glucose swings.
